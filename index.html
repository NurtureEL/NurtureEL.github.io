Nurture Early Learning is a boutique early childhood care and education service in Biloela, Queensland, where children learn through play in a calm, natural environment.

Meet Bronwyn

Nurture Early Learning is owned and directed by Bronwyn Piahana, a qualified early childhood teacher with more than 30 years' experience across kindergarten, long day care and family day care.

Bronwyn has lived and worked in Biloela for over a decade as an early childhood teacher, director and educator, and is passionate about children learning through play.

Our small setting — with places for up to 28 children — allows for genuine, personal attention, so every child is seen, heard and nurtured at their own pace.

Our Program

Learning through play, every day — indoors and out.

Play-based learning

Children follow their curiosity through open-ended, guided play that builds language, creativity and confidence.

Outdoor play & nature

Our outdoor spaces invite climbing, digging, balancing and exploring — building wonder and care for the natural world, every day.

Kindergarten program

We deliver an approved kindergarten program during school terms, building the thinking, social and self-help skills that set children up for school.

Fees & Enrolment

The details

  • Open 7:45am – 4:15pm, Monday to Friday
  • Open 50 weeks a year, closing for two weeks at Christmas
  • Places for up to 28 children aged 2 to 5
  • Approved kindergarten program during school terms

Fees

  • Daily fee of $126 for 3–5 year olds, including our kindergarten program
  • Daily fee of $137 for 2 year olds
  • Eligible kindergarten children may receive 15 funded hours a week (7.5 hours a day, up to 2 days), provided they aren't enrolled in a kindergarten program elsewhere — parents pay the gap fee for the additional hour
  • Non-refundable enrolment fee of $50 once you accept a place
  • We are an approved provider for the Child Care Subsidy (CCS) — full fees apply until your CCS is approved, and your account is credited where applicable

How to enrol

  1. Get in touch to register your interest and your preferred days.
  2. Come and visit us for a personal tour.
  3. Complete your enrolment and accept your place.
  4. Welcome to the Nurture family!
